PROJECT 01 · 2025–PRESENT

Cryptocurrency Markets and Geopolitical Risk: A Study of the Israel–Gaza Conflict

Replication and extension of a high-frequency event-study framework.

Research question

How do time-stamped geopolitical shocks associated with the Israel–Gaza conflict relate to short-horizon responses in cryptocurrency and traditional financial markets?

Approach

  • Constructed an Hourly War Risk Index (HWRI) from time-stamped geopolitical news to distinguish escalation and de-escalation shocks.
  • Consolidated 3,395 reports into 2,835 unique events and matched events with hourly market data.
  • Estimated AR, CAR and CAAR over 1–48 hour windows.
  • Applied Welch’s t-tests, effect-size analysis, and Benjamini–Hochberg FDR correction.
RESULT

Heterogeneous, short-lived, asset-specific responses were observed, with no persistent market-wide relationship over the full study period.

PROJECT 02 · 2024–2026

Knowledge Representation for Healthcare Systems: A Comparative Analysis of Performance of Different Representation Paradigms

Published research in Springer conference proceedings.

Research question

How do different knowledge-representation paradigms compare when healthcare questions require increasingly complex forms of reasoning?

Approach

  • Developed a unified framework comparing seven knowledge-representation paradigms.
  • Constructed a benchmark of 47 cardiovascular patient records.
  • Evaluated five clinical queries spanning Boolean, relational, multi-relational, and contextual reasoning.
  • Compared query coverage, reasoning depth, and explainability.
RESULT

Knowledge Graphs achieved complete coverage of all benchmark queries while supporting multi-hop and contextual reasoning.
