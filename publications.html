Knowledge Representation for Healthcare Systems: A Comparative Analysis of Performance of Different Representation Paradigms

Contribution

The work develops a unified framework for comparing seven knowledge-representation paradigms for healthcare question answering. A benchmark of 47 cardiovascular patient records and five clinical queries was used to evaluate query coverage, reasoning depth, and explainability across Boolean, relational, multi-relational, and contextual forms of reasoning.

Knowledge Graphs achieved complete coverage of the benchmark queries while supporting multi-hop and contextual reasoning.
