2025–Present

Cryptocurrency Markets and Geopolitical Risk

A study of the Israel–Gaza conflict

Replication and extension of a high-frequency event-study framework. The study constructs an Hourly War Risk Index from time-stamped geopolitical news and examines asset responses using AR, CAR and CAAR over 1–48 hour windows, together with statistical significance, effect-size analysis, and false-discovery-rate correction.

2024–2026

Knowledge Representation for Healthcare Systems

Comparative analysis of representation paradigms

A unified benchmark comparing seven knowledge-representation paradigms for healthcare question answering across Boolean, relational, multi-relational, and contextual reasoning. The study evaluates query coverage, reasoning depth, and explainability.
