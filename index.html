PUBLISHED RESEARCH · 2024–2026

Knowledge Representation for Healthcare Systems

Comparative evaluation of knowledge-representation paradigms for healthcare question answering.

The study developed a unified framework comparing seven paradigms using 47 anonymized cardiovascular patient records and five clinical queries spanning increasing levels of reasoning complexity.

ONGOING COLLABORATIVE RESEARCH · 2025–PRESENT

Geopolitical Risk and High-Frequency Financial Markets

Investigating how geopolitical shocks are reflected in cryptocurrency and traditional financial markets.

The project combines time-stamped geopolitical news with hourly financial-market data and develops an Hourly War Risk Index (HWRI). The analysis uses high-frequency event-study methodology and examines AR, CAR, and CAAR across multiple event windows.
